# Confirmatory study proposal (not run)

No additional seeds, partitions, group counts, encoder rules, or solver evaluations are used in Phase 2. The two distinct validated G2/M=8/G=2 priors establish finite-instance existence, not population advantage or broad allocation optimality.

A future independent study could freeze new source draws and the same encoder/G2 rule before any solve, retain every failure/trivial/negative row, and register prior-level rather than seed-replica counting. An allocation ablation would require a separately declared matched support-index convention and symmetric state-generation effort for Huffman, constant-length, random Kraft-compatible, and probability-sorted alternatives. It cannot be retroactively mixed with the existing registry or use its positives to select partitions.

An author decision and a new registry/commit are prerequisites. Nothing in this proposal has been evaluated or incorporated as evidence.
