# Resource terminology audit

TITLE_RECOMMENDATION: **SWITCH_TO_PARETO**

Recommended title: **Finite-Instance Pareto Certification for Group-Conditioned Discrimination of Huffman-Shaped Quantum Ensembles**.

Main lines 104--111 assign C_L=average classical Huffman length or fixed length. Smaller C_L gives larger supports at the same n_max. No monotonic relation to physical preparation cost, memory, communication, gate count or measurement effort has been established. It is an interpretable classical-length-derived encoder index, sufficient for a declared multiobjective comparison, but not an operational resource budget.

The label coordinate C_A=ceil(log2 G) has an ideal register-width interpretation. Its entropy, compression, transport and preparation costs differ and are unmodeled. Since C_A is equal, the theorem requires no exchange rate between it and C_L.

Use "declared comparison vector" or "support-index/label-width coordinates" in place of "resource vector". In the abstract's first support-index mention, explicitly identify a classical-length-derived index in a shared ambient dimension. Replace "resources are described" with "the comparison records". Pareto dominance survives this change: it concerns the declared preferences, not reduced laboratory cost.

The title currently foregrounds resource certification more strongly than the proof supports. Pareto itself must still be qualified by the coordinate definition; renaming alone does not create operational meaning. The strongest physically operational coordinate here is ideal measurement success. C_L has model semantics; it supplies a mathematical ordering, not an independently demonstrated scarce resource.

RESOURCE_TERMINOLOGY: TITLE_TOO_STRONG. Repairable with narrower terminology; not a reason to discard the mathematical pairwise certificate.
